# Initial Sources

These are human-authored educational sources and user-contributed song-chart sources. The repository stores concise paraphrases and metadata, not full copied lessons. Access date for this initial registry: 2026-11-26.

## SoundQuest — 自由派音楽理論
- Main site: https://soundquest.jp/
- Table of contents: https://soundquest.jp/quest/
- Terminology index: https://soundquest.jp/index/
- Author shown on lesson pages: yuta.
- Useful coverage: chord qualities, secondary dominants, parallel-minor borrowing, passing diminished chords, tritone substitution, slash chords, voice leading and common-tone / shortest-motion principles.
- Specific reviewed pages:
  - 二次ドミナント: https://soundquest.jp/quest/chord/chord-mv2/secondary-dominant/
  - パラレル・マイナー ❶: https://soundquest.jp/quest/chord/chord-mv2/parallel-minor/
  - パッシング・ディミニッシュ: https://soundquest.jp/quest/chord/chord-mv4/passing-chord/
  - 和声：標準連結と原則: https://soundquest.jp/quest/chord/chord-mv7/harmony-principles/
- Note: SoundQuest's own table of contents organizes material into chapters such as extension/borrowing and pop techniques. Preserve the source's terminology and distinctions when extracting.

## SRM Music / するめミュージック
- Free theory email course: https://srm-music.com/music-theory-mail-lesson/
- Course overview: https://srm-music.com/theory-summary/
- Author/creator identified on the course page: SRM.
- The free beginner course describes eight lessons covering intervals, chord construction and symbols, diatonic scales/chords, chord-scale relations, tensions and avoid notes. The page explicitly says it does not cover II-V / sub-V, modes / non-functional harmony or genre-specific composition.
- Additional reviewed articles:
  - Secondary dominant and Related II: https://srm-music.com/two-five/
  - Chord progression transformation techniques: https://srm-music.com/code/
  - Three common progression patterns: https://srm-music.com/3motions/
  - Stylish chord progression examples and techniques: https://srm-music.com/stylish-chord/
- The stylish-chord page links downloadable PDF and MIDI examples. Check download and reuse terms before bundling any files.

## Source policy
Public availability does not automatically grant redistribution rights. Do not copy full articles, lyrics, sheet music or proprietary MIDI into this repository. Keep source facts separate from project heuristics. Each knowledge record should cite its exact page, not only a site's home page.
